# Content sources

- Challenge: [Planet X and SPHEREx, NASA Space Apps 2026](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/planet-x-and-spherex/). The direct URL returned HTTP502 during research. The matching official challenge summary was available through [LSP’26](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/lsp26/) and [ExoMapper](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/exomapper/). Objective: a public-facing web tool for viewing SPHEREx observations and comparing changes over time. This page is an illustrated team presentation, not that tool.
- [NASA JPL: SPHEREx completes its first cosmic map](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-spherex-observatory-completes-first-cosmic-map-like-no-other/). Supports the 102 infrared wavelength channels and approximately six-month whole-sky mapping interval.
- [NASA Science: Hypothetical Planet X](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planet-x/). Supports describing Planet X as hypothetical and unconfirmed.
- [NASA/IPAC IRSA: SPHEREx archive](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html). Official open data portal and tools. The landing page links to the archive; it does not retrieve or display mission data.
- [NASA Science: SPHEREx](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/spherex/). Official mission background.
